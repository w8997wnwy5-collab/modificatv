# Browser evidence

`compat-dock-720.png` and `compat-list-720.png` were captured by the public-font browser regression suite of the LG port on 28 October 2026, at 1280 × 720. They use bundled public fonts, wallpaper and icons with a synthetic LG app catalogue. The suite also checks 1920 × 1080 geometry. `../images/cleanframe-dock.jpg` comes from the same run, at 1920 × 1080.

These are desktop Chromium screenshots, not TV captures or proof of how the TV's Chromium 87 composites them. They show readable labels and the rounded utility focus in the candidate UI. The wallpaper and icon sources/licences remain in the existing asset records and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
